# How to Get KeyID and KeySecret

To connect SolisCloud with Energy Ease, you need to provide the KeyID and KeySecret from SolisCloud.

For details, please refer to the [official Solis documentation](https://doc.soliscloud.com/en/20.API%20documentation/01.SolisCloud%20Platform%20API%20Document.html)

Follow the steps in **"2. API to Obtain Single Account Permission"** to get your KeyID and KeySecret.
